Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-05-13

This Privacy Policy explains how this app handles data.

1. Data We Collect

  • TikTok account authorization data (access token, refresh token, TikTok user ID)
  • Video metadata needed for publishing (caption, hashtags, publish status)
  • Basic technical logs for debugging and error tracking

2. Purpose of Processing

  • Authenticate you with TikTok
  • Upload and publish videos you request
  • Maintain operation history and improve reliability

3. Data Sharing

Data is shared only with TikTok via official TikTok API endpoints required for publishing. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

4. Data Retention

Tokens and operational data are stored only as long as necessary for app operation or until you request deletion.

5. Your Rights

  • You can revoke TikTok authorization at any time in TikTok account settings
  • You can request deletion of stored data by contacting: your-email@example.com

6. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect stored data. However, no system is completely secure.

7. Policy Changes

We may update this policy by publishing a new version on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or concerns: your-email@example.com
